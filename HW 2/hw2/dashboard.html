BuyScout.ai (operated by NetPrism Inc.)

BuyScout sells an AI Store Agent for Shopify merchants that answers shopper questions, recommends products, increases basket size, and automates routine order support across storefront and messaging channels.

What we offer

  • AI Store Agent embedded in Shopify storefronts with no-code, one-minute setup.
  • Website chat for product discovery, comparisons, recommendations, size/fit guidance, upsells, cross-sells, discounts, and FAQs.
  • Support automation for order tracking, delivery estimates, returns/refunds, troubleshooting, order modifications, subscriptions, and human handoff.
  • Multichannel shopping assistance through WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, SMS, and website chat.
  • AI voice calls for product discovery, product questions, comparisons, and verified order support.

Who we sell to

  • Shopify merchants, especially growing stores under or around 300,000 monthly visitors.
  • Direct-to-consumer and online retail brands in apparel and fashion, beauty and skincare, electronics, home and garden, health and wellness, sports and outdoors, jewelry and accessories, pets, baby and kids, and food and beverages.
  • Product categories with high-consideration purchases, fit/compatibility questions, product education needs, or meaningful cross-sell potential.
