# Seller Brief: BuyScout.ai

## Company
- **Name:** BuyScout® (BuyScout.ai)
- **Website:** https://buyscout.ai

## Why This Company
I chose BuyScout.ai because it's a company that I'm currently advising.

## What They're Offering
BuyScout® is an AI "Store Agent" for Shopify stores — an AI shopping assistant trained on a merchant's own products, policies, and store data that talks to shoppers in real time. Key aspects of the offering:

- **Core product:** A hyper-personalized AI store representative that greets visitors, answers product questions, compares products, and helps shoppers find the right fit — aiming to unblock purchases the way an attentive in-store sales associate would.
- **Value pitched to merchants:**
  - Higher conversion rates (typically cited as a 10–30% lift)
  - Larger average order value via smart upsells/cross-sells (15–25% growth)
  - Fewer support tickets (cited ~41% reduction in repetitive tickets)
- **Skills (19 total, e.g.):** Product recommendations, size & fit guidance, smart upsells, "product expert" Q&A from catalog/manuals/policies, competitor comparison, order tracking, returns & refunds.
- **Channels:** Website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Messenger, TikTok DMs, SMS — one agent deployed everywhere shoppers already are.
- **Differentiated features:**
  - AI voice calls — shoppers can talk to the agent out loud to discover products, ask follow-up questions, and get order support.
  - Virtual try-on — shoppers can see clothes/shoes/accessories rendered on their own photo/body before buying.
- **Installation/onboarding:** Installs from the Shopify App Store, auto-learns the store's catalog/policies/pages in about a minute, no-code setup.
- **Trust/compliance:** GDPR & AI Act compliant; only uses data already in the store, doesn't sell data or use it for advertising; merchants control brand voice, topic limits, and can test changes in a sandbox before they go live — notable for regulated categories like health/supplements.
- **Pricing:** Free forever under 10,000 monthly visitors; tiered visitor-based plans (Starter $49, Growth $129, Pro $249, Scale $499) up to Enterprise/custom above 300K monthly visitors.
- **Traction:** Trusted by 500+ brands; rated 4.8–5.0 across review platforms; featured customer stories include:
  - **NightCap** (health & safety — drink-spiking prevention products sold in bulk to universities, venues, brands) — 32% increase in custom-order volume.
  - **Scott Barnes Cosmetics** (beauty & cosmetics) — 22% more baskets holding 3+ products.
  - **LHS** (Taiwan-based luggage/lifestyle retailer) — 78% of eligible inquiries resolved without staff follow-up.

## Where They May Be Going in the Future
Based on the current site and product direction, likely trajectories include:
- **Expanding beyond Shopify** into other e-commerce platforms, since the current offering is Shopify-specific.
- **Deeper agentic/voice commerce** — the AI voice call feature suggests a push toward more natural, conversational (even spoken) shopping experiences beyond text chat.
- **Growth in visual/try-before-you-buy tech** — virtual try-on could expand to more product categories (footwear, accessories, home goods) and get more sophisticated (video, multi-angle).
- **Vertical specialization** — the industry-specific question sets (apparel, beauty, electronics, home & garden, health & wellness, sports, jewelry, pets, baby, food & beverage) suggest continued build-out of industry-tailored skills and compliance handling (e.g., regulated health/supplement claims).
- **Upmarket move** — the Enterprise/custom tier for 300K+ monthly visitors signals ambitions to move beyond small/mid-size Shopify merchants into larger brands.
- **More social/messaging channels** — given the existing spread across WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, and SMS, further channel additions (e.g., new social platforms) seem likely as commerce continues to move into chat and DMs.
